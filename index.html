Spring on Route 7

The jacarandas on Ridgeview Rd are out and the depot smells like mown grass. A big thank you to everyone who's signed up for key-holding. Forty of you now trust us with a spare, and we don't take that lightly. I'm only five foot two, but I can still load a van faster than the young ones.

Our very first delivery went out in the spring of 1987, before we even had a sign on the door. A little one. I've never forgotten it.

My Neil has been Employee of the Month again. I'm not biased, the customers vote! He's always been a homebody, my boy. Happiest in other people's houses, fixing a dripping tap or putting a parcel somewhere safe.

If you see him, give him a wave. He'll already know it's you.

Margaret
